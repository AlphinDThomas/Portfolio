ML System for Dysarthric Speech

Designed and developed an Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) system that improves the intelligibility of dysarthric speech — making technology more accessible for speech-impaired users. Fine-tuned OpenAI's Whisper Base model on the TORGO dysarthric speech dataset using parameter-efficient techniques.

  • Fine-tuned Whisper Base using dysarthric speech audio from the TORGO dataset
  • Applied PEFT with LoRA architecture — only ~200,000 trainable parameters needed
  • Enabled accurate conversion of dysarthric speech to text, drastically improving accessibility

Outperformed OpenAI's Whisper Medium — a significantly larger model — on dysarthric audio transcription benchmarks, despite having far fewer parameters.

PulseGuard: Identity Verification Pipeline

Developed a zero-trust electronic Know Your Customer (e-KYC) onboarding pipeline engineered to prevent remote verification fraud through deepfakes. Rather than standard face matching, PulseGuard utilizes a multi-dimensional biometric validation architecture.

  • Identity Anchor: Compares active camera captures against high-dimensional facial embeddings securely logged inside cloud databases.
  • Passive Physiological Check: Tracks vascular micro-color pixel stability across facial surfaces to verify human heartbeat presence via rPPG math.
  • Cognitive Challenge Engine: Defeats recorded video inject attacks by prompting randomized spatial finger count inputs classified via MediaPipe Hands.
  • Spatial Awareness Metric: Maps 3D Perspective-n-Point orientations to force sudden head angles, exposing synthetic occlusion deepfake artifacts.
